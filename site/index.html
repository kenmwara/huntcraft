    New to hunting? This is how it works.

    This is a manual, not an answer key. Each flag walks you through where to look and why, then one query at a time. First, set Defender's time zone to UTC (Get your bearings, step 2 shows exactly how): every time in the manual is UTC. Then run each query yourself in Defender and you'll reach the answer on your own. Check your answer when you have one. Reveal is there if you're truly stuck.
